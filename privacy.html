Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Chairside helps dental practices hear from their patients. This page explains what we collect when you scan a practice's QR code.

What we collect

  • Your message, if you send one.
  • Your name and phone or email, only if you choose to give them (optional).
  • Anonymous counts: how many times the code was scanned and which button was tapped. These aren't linked to who you are.

How we use it

Your message and contact details go only to the practice whose code you scanned, so they can get back to you and put things right. We don't sell them, publish them, or use them for advertising.

Who sees it

Only that practice, and Chairside as the service provider. Data is stored on Cloudflare's servers, and messages are delivered to the practice by email (sent through Resend).

Please don't include more medical detail in your message than you need to explain the problem.

How long we keep it, and your rights

We keep data for as long as the practice uses Chairside. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to buildwithoris@gmail.com.
