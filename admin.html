Everyone you add below appears here automatically. Pause switches a clinic off straight away: its QR page stops working, patients can't send notes, and the dentist can't log in to the dashboard. Resume switches everything back on — nothing is deleted. To delete a clinic for good, pause it first; a Remove… button then appears.
